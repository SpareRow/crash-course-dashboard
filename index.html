Reading these numbers

  • These are proxies, not sales. Real point-of-sale numbers come from BookScan (PRH Author Portal) and BookNet Canada.
  • Indigo shelf drawdown counts copies that left named stores since the previous day: mostly sales, plus some transfers and returns. A same-day restock hides sales, so read weekly totals.
  • Indigo warehouse & online is stock at locations that aren't stores (distribution centres, online fulfilment, plus a few stores not yet identified).
  • Barnes & Noble only shows whether a store has the book, not how many copies.
  • Collected once a day from public retailer pages.
